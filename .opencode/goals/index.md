## Estados
- `active` — en curso, evaluado contra drift
- `done` — success_criteria verificados, archivado
- `dropped` — abandonado con razón en archive

## Uso
- `/skill_goal` — crear/anclar
- `/skill_goal status` — ver activo
- `/skill_goal recall <query>` — buscar en goals
- `/skill_goal close` / `/skill_goal drop <razón>` — cerrar
